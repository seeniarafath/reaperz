COMMUNITY STANDARDS

REAPERZ RULES

Respect the brotherhood, uphold community honor, and keep every session competitive and fair.

01 — MUTUAL RESPECT

Treat all members with respect. Hate speech, discrimination, personal insults, and needless toxicity will result in an immediate permanent ban.

02 — ROLEPLAY INTEGRITY

Adhere strictly to FiveM roleplay standards. Value life, do not RDM/VDM, stay in character during serious RP scenarios, and support immersive storylines.

03 — FAIR PLAY & ANTI-CHEAT

Cheating, exploiting game bugs, using unauthorized mod menus, crosshair overlays, or external scripts is strictly prohibited across all crew activities.

04 — PRIVACY & SECURITY

Never disclose any member's real identity, location, social accounts, or private communications without their explicit permission.

05 — STAFF AUTHORITY & DISPUTES

Follow instructions from Founders, Admins, and Moderators. In case of conflicts, use the Discord ticket channel rather than creating public drama.

06 — PROUD TAMIL GAMING CULTURE

Keep the server welcoming, vibrant, and enjoyable for Tamil gamers worldwide, whether newcomers or seasoned veterans.
