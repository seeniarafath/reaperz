OUR ORIGIN & MISSION

BUILT ON PASSION. BOUND BY LOYALTY.

Founded by Dark, REAPERZ was born from a simple yet powerful dream: to bring Tamil gamers from across the globe into one cohesive, high-energy gaming brotherhood. Whether hailing from Tamil Nadu, Sri Lanka, Malaysia, Singapore, the UAE, or Europe, Tamil gamers have found a true home within our community.

What began as an elite squad executing high-stakes GTA Online heists has evolved into a thriving multi-faceted gaming organization. Today, REAPERZ stands as a benchmark for immersive FiveM Tamil roleplay, organized crew operations, high-speed street racing meets, and vibrant voice communications.

We take pride in fostering a zero-toxicity atmosphere where newcomers receive patient mentorship, seasoned veterans find worthy allies, and every gaming session is packed with laughter, adrenaline, and authentic Tamil culture.
